TK growth is closing Bay Area preschools fast
Free universal TK is pulling 4-year-olds from private preschools. Berkeley and Crockett programs have already closed, shrinking options for families with younger children.
Implications / For parents
→Explore TK enrollment at your district now — spots fill early in the fall
→Watch for closures if your child under 4 relies on a community preschool

California TK has no set curriculum — play-based math wins
California's TK has no mandated curriculum statewide. Research shows play-based math — counting steps, sorting shapes — builds the foundation kids need through elementary school.
Implications / For parents
→Consider counting steps, sorting toys, or spotting patterns on your next walk
→Ask your child's TK teacher how math is woven into daily play and routines
